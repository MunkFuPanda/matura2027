# 🎬 Pitch: Social Media Campaign for the IT Department

## Goal

Show students and parents how exciting, creative, and valuable studying IT at our school is – through short, engaging TikTok/Reels-style videos.

---

## Why This Matters

* **For Students:** IT looks fun, modern, and connected to their everyday lives (games, apps, coding, teamwork).
* **For Parents:** IT education provides future-proof career opportunities and practical problem-solving skills.
* **For the School:** Improves the image of the IT department, attracts more interest, and shows we are forward-thinking.

---

## Concept: Short-Form Videos

* **Format:** 20–40 seconds, quick cuts, captions, upbeat music.
* **Style:** Fun, relatable, professional but not overproduced.
* **Channels:** TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts (school account).

### Example Ideas

1. *Expectation vs. Reality of Coding* → Matrix-style typing vs. actual coding with cheering.
2. *From Gamer to Coder* → student plays a game, then codes their own.
3. *Teamwork in IT* → fast shots of students collaborating and laughing.
4. *Future Proof* → student says: “I’m learning skills companies hire for.”

---

## Execution

* **Filming:** Students record on phones (no extra cost).
* **Editing:** Free/simple tools (CapCut, DaVinci Resolve).
* **Frequency:** 1–2 videos per week over a few weeks.
* **Teacher’s Role:** Approve filming, give access to labs/classrooms, check alignment with school’s social media policy.

---

## Benefits

* Engages current students as ambassadors.
* Promotes IT as creative and future-oriented.
* Strengthens the school’s image in the community.

---

**Slogan:**

> *“Build the Future. Code Your World.”*
