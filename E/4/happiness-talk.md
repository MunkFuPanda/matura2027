**What happiness means to me:**

* Not constant joy, but balance and inner peace
* Feeling comfortable with who you are
* Found in small, everyday moments (music, friends, quiet time)
* Accepting imperfection instead of chasing perfection

**What makes it easy/hard for our generation to be happy:**

* Easy:

  * Access to information and opportunities
  * Global connection through technology
  * Freedom of expression and creativity

* Hard:

  * Negative input from news and social media
  * Constant comparison on social media
  * Pressure to succeed early in life
  * Uncertainty about the future (climate, economy, politics)

**Ways to increase happiness (personal & societal):**

* Personal level:

  * Limit social media use
  * Practice gratitude and mindfulness
  * Talk openly about emotions
  * Focus on real friendships and hobbies
  * Set realistic goals and celebrate small wins
  * Stay in touch with many people

* Societal level:

  * Schools should teach emotional intelligence and stress management
  * Promote mental health awareness and support systems
  * Create safe spaces for young people to express themselves

**Conclusion:**

* Happiness is built daily — not found once
* Comes from kindness, honesty, and balance
